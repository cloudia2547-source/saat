The problem

Events end. The litter stays.

Chatuchak at closing time, Big Mountain after the last set, Rajamangala at full time. Each ends the same way: a thinning crowd, full hands, and bins that are too far away to bother with.

Bins stand where people aren't

Fixed bins sit at the edges of a venue. Litter piles up in the middle, where people actually eat, drink and wait.

Cleanup starts after everyone leaves

Crews arrive once the venue is empty. By then wrappers have blown into drains and food waste has been trodden into the ground.

Nothing changes the habit

At the moment someone decides where their cup goes, nothing nudges them toward the bin. The same mess repeats at the next event.

KPIs for success

Two numbers decide if it works.

One measures behaviour, one measures the ground. The simulation tracks both live, with and without s'aat on site.
